🏥 Smart Healthcare, Simplified

Smart Healthcare Portal brings patients, doctors, administrators and laboratory technicians together on one digital platform.

The system makes it easier to manage appointments, medical information, prescriptions and laboratory services from one place.

📅

Online Appointments

Patients can book appointments with registered doctors and manage their appointments through the portal.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor Services

Registered doctors can access their dashboard and manage patient-related healthcare information.

🩸

Blood Test Booking

Patients can request blood tests and choose home sample collection when available.

🧪

Laboratory Reports

Blood test results entered by the laboratory technician can be accessed by the patient.

📋

Medical Records

Important medical information can be maintained digitally for easier access and management.

💊

Prescriptions

Patients can view prescriptions provided by their doctors through their dashboard.

👨‍🔬

Laboratory Management

The appointed laboratory technician can review blood test requests and manage test reports.

👨‍💼

Administration

Administrators can manage doctors, patients, appointments, blood tests and system information.
