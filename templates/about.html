About Our Portal

Smart Healthcare Portal is a digital healthcare platform designed to bring important healthcare services together in one convenient system.

The portal connects patients, doctors, administrators and laboratory technicians, making healthcare information and services easier to manage.

📅

Appointments

Patients can book and manage appointments with registered doctors.

📋

Medical Records

Patients can access their medical information through the portal.

💊

Prescriptions

Patients can view prescriptions provided by their doctors.

🩸

Blood Tests

Patients can request blood tests with home sample collection services.

🧪

Lab Reports

Completed blood test reports can be viewed by patients through the portal.

👨‍⚕️

Healthcare Professionals

Doctors and laboratory technicians can manage healthcare-related information through their respective dashboards.
